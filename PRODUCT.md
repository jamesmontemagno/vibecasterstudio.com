# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Independent podcasters and small creator teams who record remote conversations from their desktop browsers. They need guests to join without friction and need confidence that a weak connection or browser crash will not erase an irreplaceable take.

## Product Purpose

VibeCaster Studio is a browser-based remote audio and video recording studio. It makes studio-quality remote recording approachable while protecting each participant's local, isolated capture with continuous resumable cloud backup. Success means creators can focus on the conversation, recover when connections fail, and leave with flexible tracks and exports ready for the next step.

## Brand Personality

Creative, playful, expressive, and dependable. The voice should feel like a capable producer beside the creator: warm and encouraging during setup, calm and exact when discussing recording or recovery.

Zencastr is a positive product-flow reference for its approachable, podcast-first experience.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a dense broadcast control room, a generic corporate video meeting, or a complex professional editor. Avoid cream or beige AI landing pages, gradients, gradient text, glassmorphism, generic SaaS metrics, repetitive feature-card grids, ubiquitous uppercase eyebrows, stock conference imagery, and visual promises that the product cannot substantiate.

## Design Principles

1. **Show resilience, not infrastructure.** Make local recording and recovery understandable through a memorable visual story rather than technical architecture.
2. **Keep the creator in the conversation.** Explain benefits in the language of episodes, guests, takes, and tracks.
3. **Let expressive moments earn trust.** Use playful composition and color around a clear hierarchy; become calm and precise whenever data safety is discussed.
4. **Make readiness feel effortless.** Guest access and preflight should read as a smooth arrival, not a setup checklist.
5. **Stay truthful while early.** Present the product direction clearly without fabricated proof, pricing, deadlines, or calls to action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve strong text and non-text contrast, logical landmarks and heading order, visible keyboard focus, browser zoom and text resizing, and meaningful alternatives for visual content. Do not rely on color alone. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep the complete experience available without animation or JavaScript.
