# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Independent podcasters recording remote conversations. They need a dependable session setup that is easy to run themselves and comfortable for guests.

## Product Purpose

Rill & Pine is a remote podcast-recording product. It makes reliable recordings easy to capture, even when a guest has an unstable internet connection.

## Positioning

Rill & Pine leads with dependable local recordings, then supports that promise with an intentionally focused recording room and a calm, guided session flow for hosts and guests.

## Operating Context

Hosts invite remote guests into recording sessions and need to focus on the conversation rather than technical setup or recovery from connection problems.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Remote podcast conversations.
- Reliable local recording designed to withstand weak internet connections.
- A focused recording room with essential controls only.
- A guided, guest-friendly session flow.
- The product intentionally avoids flashy or AI-led features.
- The launch site collects waitlist signups through a hosted Tally form.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Rill & Pine.
- Voice: calm, memorable, and dependable.
- Regional cue: understated Pacific Northwest character.
- The brand communicates simplicity and stability, not artificial intelligence or novelty.

## Evidence on Hand

No brand assets, customer testimonials, pricing, benchmarks, or approved product screenshots are present in the repository. Future launch work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Make reliability tangible and easy to understand.
2. Keep the host and guest focused on the conversation.
3. Prefer calm clarity over feature density.
4. Earn trust with specific product behavior, never inflated claims.
