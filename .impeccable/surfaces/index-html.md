# Rill & Pine launch page

## Scope and mode

Single-page **Persuade** surface for an early-stage waitlist launch.

## Audience, job, and action

Independent podcast hosts arrive evaluating whether a remote recording tool can keep a conversation safe when a connection is weak. The action is joining the waitlist.

## Proof and constraints

The page demonstrates the local-recording resilience concept through a clearly labelled illustrative session instrument. There are no approved testimonials, metrics, pricing, product screenshots, or customer logos. Do not fabricate them or make AI the product story.

## Chosen direction

**The River-Gauge Field Station.** A rain-dark, Pacific Northwest instrument world frames connection instability as a manageable condition. The memorable moment is the session instrument changing from steady flow to rough weather while its local recording state remains protected.

## Interaction and layout

Hero proposition and signup pair with the session instrument at desktop widths; the content stacks at smaller widths. The waitlist is hosted by Tally, so its submission, confirmation, and validation states are owned by the provider. Motion is limited to a useful condition change and must respect reduced-motion preferences.
